# Competitor Analysis: SEO Keyword Strategy

## Keyword Strategy

### The Reality Check

Outrank ranks for 1,547 keywords but most of their traffic comes from:

- Branded searches ("outrank")
- SEO tool comparisons ("surfer seo vs ahrefs", "moz vs ahrefs")
- Generic blog content ("blog writing examples")

They're **NOT** ranking for the obvious "AI SEO content" keywords. That's your opportunity.

### Primary Keywords to Target

**High Intent, Moderate Competition:**

| Keyword                         | Why Target                                  |
| ------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| "AI SEO content generator"      | Outrank doesn't rank for their own category |
| "automated blog writing tool"   | Direct product-market fit                   |
| "AI content writer for SEO"     | Combines AI + SEO intent                    |
| "SEO autopilot tool"            | Outrank's tagline, but they don't own it    |
| "automatic blog post generator" | High purchase intent                        |

### Competitor Capture Keywords

These are your fastest wins — people actively looking for alternatives:

| Keyword                   | Opportunity                                     |
| ------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| "Outrank alternative"     | Capture dissatisfied users (quality complaints) |
| "Byword alternative"      | Their support/hosting issues = your gain        |
| "Jasper for SEO"          | Jasper users wanting SEO-specific tool          |
| "Frase alternative"       | Users wanting automation, not manual workflows  |
| "cheaper than Surfer SEO" | Price-sensitive segment                         |
| "RankYak vs Outrank"      | Insert yourself into emerging comparisons       |

### Comparison Keywords

This is how Outrank gets 30%+ of their traffic. Copy the playbook:

**Create Content For:**

- "[Your Brand] vs Outrank"
- "[Your Brand] vs Byword"
- "[Your Brand] vs Jasper"
- "Surfer SEO vs Frase vs [Your Brand]"
- "Best AI SEO tools 2025" (listicle, rank yourself)
- "Outrank review" (honest take, pitch yourself)

### Problem-Aware Keywords

People searching for solutions, not tools yet:

| Keyword                                | Search Intent                               |
| -------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| "AI content that ranks on Google"      | Outcome-focused                             |
| "how to automate blog content"         | DIY searchers ready for tool                |
| "SEO content without sounding like AI" | Quality-conscious buyers                    |
| "automated backlink building"          | Outrank's backlink feature = differentiator |
| "how to scale blog content"            | Agency/startup pain point                   |
| "content that passes AI detection"     | Growing concern                             |

### Niche Vertical Keywords (Low Competition, High Conversion)

Pick **ONE** vertical and dominate it:

**E-commerce:**

- "AI product descriptions for Shopify"
- "automated SEO for e-commerce"
- "Shopify blog automation"
- "WooCommerce AI content"

**SaaS:**

- "AI blog writer for SaaS"
- "B2B content automation"
- "SaaS SEO content tool"

**Agencies:**

- "white label AI content"
- "AI SEO tool for agencies"
- "bulk SEO content generation"

### Keywords to AVOID

Don't waste resources competing here:

| Keyword                 | Why Skip                                   |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| "SEO tools"             | Too broad, dominated by Ahrefs/SEMrush     |
| "AI writer"             | Jasper/Copy.ai own this, not SEO-specific  |
| "content optimization"  | Surfer/Frase territory, DR 80+ competitors |
| "keyword research tool" | Ahrefs, SEMrush, Ubersuggest dominate      |

## Content Priority Order

### Month 1:

1. "[Brand] vs Outrank" comparison page
2. "Best AI SEO Content Generators 2025" listicle
3. "Outrank Alternative" landing page
4. "Byword Alternative" landing page

### Month 2:

5. "How to Automate SEO Content" guide
6. "[Brand] vs Byword vs Frase" comparison
7. "AI Content That Ranks" case study
8. Pick your vertical — 3-4 niche keyword pages

### Month 3:

9. "Jasper vs [Brand] for SEO"
10. Problem-aware content (5-10 blog posts)
11. Double down on what's ranking

## TL;DR

| Priority | Keywords                                        | Goal                     |
| -------- | ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| #1       | Competitor alternatives ("Outrank alternative") | Capture unhappy users    |
| #2       | Comparison posts ("[Brand] vs X")               | Steal competitor traffic |
| #3       | Category keywords ("AI SEO content generator")  | Own the category         |
| #4       | One vertical (e-commerce OR SaaS)               | Dominate a niche         |

**Outrank left the obvious keywords on the table. Take them.**
