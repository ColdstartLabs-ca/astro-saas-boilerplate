## Recommended Content Roadmap (Months 1–6)

**Month 1–2 (Foundation):**
Build 4–6 cornerstone pages targeting your brand-defining terms: homepage for "autopilot SEO," a product page for "automated rank tracking," and a "what is automated SEO" guide. These establish topical authority and are the core of your internal linking structure.

**Month 2–3 (Quick Win Content):**
Publish blogs on the lowest-KD, highest-relevance keywords: "automated SEO tools," "semantic SEO automation," "how to track Google rankings," and "keyword research examples." These mirror what outrank.so and rankyak.com validated, but you can outdo them with more depth and tool-specific context.

**Month 3–4 (Comparison Pages):**
Launch 3–5 comparison posts in the rank tracker / SEO automation category. These convert well commercially and build DR as people link to comparison content.

**Month 4–6 (Informational Volume):**
Go after high-volume, low-KD informational terms like the "search google or type a url" cluster and "blog examples" type content if you want raw traffic — but only if this serves your funnel (e.g., as top-of-funnel for retargeting). If your audience is pure SEO professionals, focus more on the SEO-specific informational content above.

---

## Key Tactical Notes

**Avoid immediately:** Keywords with KD 50+ (like "link building packages" KD 35, "seo reseller program" KD 71, "google rank tracking software" KD 53). These require significant domain authority that you don't have yet.

**Topical focus beats breadth:** Both competitors dilute themselves with loosely related content (QuillBot reviews, blog-writing-for-students). autopilotrank.com should stay tightly focused on the automated SEO / rank tracking cluster. Google rewards topical authority, especially for new sites.

**Free tool pages work:** outrank.so gets consistent traffic from their LSI Keyword Generator and Blog Outline Generator tool pages. Consider building 1–2 free mini-tools (e.g., a "Rank Tracking Spreadsheet Template" or a "Keyword Position Checker") — these attract backlinks naturally and rank for low-KD tool-intent queries.

**Target rankyak's weaknesses:** rankyak.com ranks for "white label SEO reseller" and "link building packages" — neither of which aligns with your autopilot/automated positioning. Don't chase those. Instead, exploit the "automated SEO" cluster where rankyak is present but weak (they rank for "automated SEO software" and "semantic SEO automation" but with low traffic), and your brand name is a stronger fit.
