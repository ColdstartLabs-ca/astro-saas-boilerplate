## 1. Awareness Channels

### Primary Awareness Channels

#### Content Marketing & SEO (Owned)

**Strategy**: Rank for high-intent keywords that our target customers search for.

| Target Keywords             | Intent        | Competitor                |
| --------------------------- | ------------- | ------------------------- |
| "programmatic SEO software" | Commercial    | Surfer, Jasper            |
| "automated blog writing"    | Commercial    | Copy.ai, Jasper           |
| "SEO content automation"    | Commercial    | Scalenut, Frase           |
| "bulk content generation"   | Commercial    | Writesonic, Article Forge |
| "AI content writer for SEO" | Commercial    | All competitors           |
| "programmatic SEO agency"   | Commercial    | Agencies                  |
| "scale SEO content"         | Informational | Bloggers, SEO guides      |
| "SEO content outsourcing"   | Commercial    | Agencies, Fiverr          |

**Content Types**:

- Blog posts on SEO strategies, programmatic SEO techniques
- Comparison guides (vs. Jasper, Surfer, etc.)
- Case studies showing traffic growth
- Free tools (keyword clustering, content brief generator)
- Templates (content calendars, SEO checklists)

**Metrics**:

- Organic traffic growth: 50% YoY
- Keyword rankings: Top 10 for 500+ target keywords
- Backlinks: 50+ DR50+ links annually

#### YouTube Channel (Owned)

**Strategy**: Educational content showcasing the platform and building authority.

**Content Pillars**:

1. **Programmatic SEO Tutorials**: How-to guides on pSEO strategies
2. **Tool Comparisons**: Honest reviews vs. competitors
3. **Case Studies**: Real results from users
4. **Platform Demos**: Feature walkthroughs

**Target Channels/Playlists**:

- SEO niche (Ahrefs, Moz, SEMrush audiences)
- SaaS founders (Indie Hackers, MicroConf communities)
- Content marketing communities

**KPIs**:

- Subscribers: 10K+ in year 1
- Views: 100K+ monthly by month 12
- Click-through to site: 3-5%

#### Product Hunt & Launch Platforms (Earned)

**Strategy**: Launch on Product Hunt and similar platforms for burst awareness.

**Platforms**:

- Product Hunt (main launch)
- Hacker News (Show HN)
- Indie Hackers (launch post)
- BetaList (beta phase)
- There's An AI For That (AI tool directory)

**Launch Strategy**:

- Build email list pre-launch
- Recruit hunters and upvoters from community
- Offer launch-exclusive deals
- Prepare assets: demo video, screenshots, copy

**Target KPI**:

- Product Hunt: Top 5 Product of the Day
- 500+ signups from launch week

#### Podcast Guest Appearances (Earned)

**Strategy**: Appear on podcasts our target customers listen to.

**Target Podcasts**:

- **For Agencies**: Agency Life, The Digital Agency Show, Savvy Farmer
- **For SEO**: Search Engine Journal, Authority Hacker, Marketing School
- **For SaaS**: SaaS Breakdown, MicroConf, Indie Hackers
- **For Content Marketing**: Content Marketing Institute, Copyblogger FM

**Pitch Angle**:

- "How we built an autonomous SEO content platform"
- "The future of programmatic SEO"
- "How to scale content 10x without hiring"

**KPI**:

- 1-2 podcast appearances per month
- Trackable signups from promo codes

#### Paid Advertising (Paid)

**Google Search Ads**:

- Target competitor brand names + "alternative"
- High-intent keywords (bottom of funnel)
- Budget: $5-10K/month initially

**LinkedIn Ads**:

- Target agency owners, marketing directors
- Sponsored content + conversation ads
- Budget: $3-5K/month

**YouTube Ads**:

- Target competitor video viewers
- Tutorial/intent-based targeting
- Budget: $2-3K/month

**Retargeting**:

- Site visitors, YouTube viewers
- Cross-platform retargeting

**KPIs**:

- CAC under $100 for SMB segment
- CAC under $300 for agency segment

## 2. Consideration Channels

### Email Marketing (Owned)

**Strategy**: Nurture leads through educational content and social proof.

**Email Sequences**:

1. **Welcome Sequence (5 emails)**:
   - Email 1: Welcome + case study
   - Email 2: How programmatic SEO works
   - Email 3: Customer success story
   - Email 4: Comparison vs. alternatives
   - Email 5: Free trial/demo offer

2. **Webinar Sequence**:
   - Invite to live training
   - Reminder emails
   - Replay + offer

3. **Abandoned Cart**:
   - Return to complete signup
   - Social proof
   - FAQ objection handling

**Lead Magnets**:

- Free programmatic SEO template
- Keyword clustering tool (free tier)
- Content quality checklist
- Mini-course: "Programmatic SEO in 7 Days"

**KPIs**:

- Open rate: 30%+
- Click rate: 3-5%
- Unsubscribe rate: <1%

#### Webinars & Live Training (Owned)

**Weekly Live Training**: "Programmatic SEO Workshop"

- How to set up programmatic SEO campaigns
- Live demos of the platform
- Q&A session
- Exclusive offer for attendees

**Monthly Case Study Webinar**:

- Deep dive into customer success
- Interview with customer
- Tactics and results
- Q&A

**KPIs**:

- Registration rate: 10-15% of email list
- Attendance rate: 30-40% of registrants
- Attendee-to-trial conversion: 15-20%

#### Case Studies & Testimonials (Owned)

**Format**: Written case studies + video testimonials

**Distribution**:

- Website case studies page
- Email to prospects
- Social media snippets
- Sales collateral

**Types**:

- SMB: "How I got 10K monthly visitors in 3 months"
- Agency: "How we 5x'd our client content capacity"
- E-commerce: "Product content automation that increased organic revenue 40%"

**KPI**:

- 2+ new case studies per month
- Video testimonial from every enterprise customer

#### Comparison Pages (Owned)

**Strategy**: Rank for [Competitor] vs. AutopilotRank searches.

**Pages to Create**:

- Jasper vs. AutopilotRank
- Surfer SEO vs. AutopilotRank
- Copy.ai vs. AutopilotRank
- Scalenut vs. AutopilotRank
- Frase vs. AutopilotRank
- Article Forge vs. AutopilotRank
- AutopilotRank alternatives

**Content Structure**:

- Feature comparison table
- Pricing comparison
- Use case fit
- Pros/cons of each
- When to choose which

**KPI**:

- Rank top 5 for all comparison keywords
- 500+ monthly visits from comparison pages

## Channel Roadmap

### Phase 1 (Months 1-6): Foundation

- Launch blog and SEO program
- Set up email marketing infrastructure
- Create initial free tools/lead magnets
- Launch on Product Hunt

### Phase 2 (Months 7-12): Scale

- YouTube channel launch
- Podcast guest appearances (1-2/month)
- Paid advertising pilot ($5-10K/month)
- Agency partner program launch

### Phase 3 (Year 2): Optimize

- Expand paid to $20-30K/month
- Launch affiliate program
- Marketplace listings (Shopify, etc.)
- International localization

### Phase 4 (Year 3): Diversify

- Direct sales team for enterprise
- Strategic partnerships
- Reseller program expansion
- Events and conference presence
