# Revenue Streams

## Overview

AutopilotRank generates revenue through a SaaS subscription model with tiered pricing based on usage (articles generated) and features. This document outlines all revenue streams, pricing strategy, and monetization approach.

---

## Primary Revenue Streams

### 1. SaaS Subscriptions (80-90% of revenue)

The core revenue driver - recurring subscriptions for platform access.

#### Pricing Tiers

| Tier           | Price/Month | Price/Year       | Articles/Month | Key Features                                 | Target Segment                      |
| -------------- | ----------- | ---------------- | -------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| **Free**       | $0          | -                | 10 articles    | Basic features, community support            | Try before buying, very small users |
| **Pro**        | $49         | $468 ($39/mo)    | 100 articles   | All core features, priority support          | SMBs, content site owners           |
| **Business**   | $199        | $1,908 ($159/mo) | 500 articles   | Team access, API access, white-label         | Agencies, growing teams             |
| **Enterprise** | Custom      | Custom           | Unlimited      | SSO, custom integrations, dedicated CSM, SLA | Enterprise, high-volume users       |

**Annual Discount**: ~20% for annual prepayment

#### Tier Rationale

**Free Tier (Acquisition)**:

- Purpose: Let users experience value without risk
- 10 articles = enough to see results, not enough to rely on
- No credit card required = lower friction
- Converts 10-15% to paid within 30 days

**Pro Tier (Core Revenue)**:

- Sweet spot pricing: $49/mo is affordable for SMBs
- 100 articles/mo = 3-4 articles/day (solo content schedule)
- Features: Everything needed for successful campaigns
- Margin: ~85% after infrastructure costs

**Business Tier (Expansion)**:

- For teams and agencies
- 500 articles/mo = supports multiple clients or campaigns
- Team features: Collaboration, white-label
- API access: For custom integrations
- Margin: ~80% (higher support costs)

**Enterprise Tier (Custom)**:

- Starting at $1,000-5,000/month
- Custom pricing based on:
  - Article volume needs
  - Team size
  - Integration requirements
  - Support level
  - SLA requirements
- Margin: ~70% (higher service delivery costs)

### 2. Usage-Based Overage Charges (5-10% of revenue)

For customers who need occasional bursts beyond their plan.

#### Overage Pricing

| Tier       | Overage Rate  | Explanation                     |
| ---------- | ------------- | ------------------------------- |
| Pro        | $0.75/article | 50% above per-article base rate |
| Business   | $0.50/article | 25% above per-article base rate |
| Enterprise | Custom        | Negotiated based on volume      |

**Rationale**:

- Encourages plan upgrades for consistent overage
- Flexible for seasonal needs
- Creates urgency to upgrade for cost savings

**Example**:

- Pro customer (100 articles) generates 150 articles
- First 100 included in plan
- Next 50 at $0.75 = $37.50 overage
- Upsell: Upgrade to Business (500 articles) for only $150 more

## Pricing Strategy

### Pricing Philosophy

**Value-Based Pricing**:

- Price based on value delivered, not cost-plus
- Compare to cost of alternatives (hiring writers = $50-200/article)
- 100 articles for $49 = $0.49/article (99% cheaper than alternatives)

**Tiered Usage**:

- Simple, transparent tiers
- Overage pricing for flexibility
- Annual prepay discount for cash flow

**Competitive Positioning**:

- Mid-market pricing (not cheapest, not most expensive)
- Premium to AI generators (more value)
- Fraction of agency costs

### Pricing Psychology

**Anchoring**:

- Show Business/Enterprise prices first
- Make Pro seem like a great deal

**Decoy Effect**:

- Annual pricing = clearly better value
- Tier pricing makes next tier look attainable

**Loss Aversion**:

- "Save $X/year" messaging
- Limited-time offers

### Discount Policies

**Standard Discounts**:

- Annual prepayment: 20% off
- Non-profit: 25% off
- Education: 30% off

**Sales Discounts**:

- End of quarter: Up to 20% for Enterprise deals
- Multi-year: Additional 10% for 2-year commits
- Volume: Custom pricing for 100+ seats

**No Discounts**:

- Never discount on monthly plans
- Never discount below Pro tier ($39/mo)
- Never discount for enterprise without multi-year or volume

### Billing Methods

| Method                 | Availability | Take Rate |
| ---------------------- | ------------ | --------- |
| **Credit Card**        | All plans    | 85%       |
| **Direct Debit (ACH)** | Business+    | 10%       |
| **Invoice/Wire**       | Enterprise   | 5%        |
| **Crypto**             | N/A          | 0%        |

### Payment Processing

**Primary**: Stripe

- Supports all major cards
- Handles international payments
- Built-in fraud protection
- Subscription management

**Secondary**: Stripe Connect for partners

- Commission payouts
- Partner accounts
- Marketplace functionality

### Collection Strategy

**Dunning Process**:

- Day 1: Payment failed, friendly retry notification
- Day 3: Automatic retry
- Day 5: Second retry + "update card" email
- Day 7: Final retry + account suspension warning
- Day 10: Account suspended (content paused)
- Day 30: Account canceled

**Recovery Rates**:

- Day 1-3: 70% recovered
- Day 3-7: 20% recovered
- Day 7-30: 5% recovered
- Beyond Day 30: 5% recovered (manual outreach)

## Revenue Recognition

### ASC 606 Compliance

**Subscription Revenue**:

- Recognized ratably over subscription period
- Not recognized upfront (even for annual prepay)
- Deferred revenue liability on balance sheet

**Setup Fees**:

- Recognized over subscription term
- Not all at signup

**Professional Services**:

- Recognized when delivered
- Separate from subscription

### Key Metrics for SaaS

**MRR (Monthly Recurring Revenue)**:

- Base metric for business health
- New MRR + Expansion MRR - Churn MRR = Net New MRR

**ARR (Annual Recurring Revenue)**:

- MRR × 12
- Used for valuation, benchmarking

**Bookings**:

- Total value of contracts signed
- Includes both recurring and non-recurring

**Billings**:

- Cash collected from customers
- Important for cash flow management

## Revenue Optimization

### A/B Testing Priorities

1. **Pricing Page Layout**
   - 3 tiers vs. 4 tiers
   - Annual toggle placement
   - Feature emphasis

2. **Free Trial Length**
   - 7 days vs. 14 days
   - Article limit (10 vs. 20)
   - Credit card required vs. not required

3. **Annual Discount**
   - 15% vs. 20% vs. 25%
   - Messaging ("Save 20%" vs. "2 months free")

4. **Upgrade Triggers**
   - Article limit messaging
   - Feature unlock prompting
   - Timing of prompts

### Expansion Revenue Strategy

**In-Product Triggers**:

- 80% of plan used → gentle upgrade reminder
- Plan limit hit → hard block with upgrade CTA
- Feature request → if it's in higher tier, show upgrade path

**Email Triggers**:

- Monthly usage summary → "You're close to your limit"
- Success email → "Scale this success with upgrade"
- Feature announcement → "Available in higher tiers"

**Sales Touch**:

- For Business+ customers
- Quarterly business reviews
- Expansion opportunities discussion

### Churn Reduction = Revenue Protection

Every 1% reduction in churn = significant ARR impact.

**Focus Areas**:

- Onboarding success (first month is highest churn risk)
- Ongoing value delivery (usage-based success)
- Proactive retention (identify at-risk customers)
- Win-back campaigns (targeted offers)
