# Customer Relationships

## Overview

This document outlines how AutopilotRank acquires, retains, and grows relationships with customers across different segments.

## Relationship Types by Segment

### 1. Self-Service (SMBs, Content Site Owners)

**Type**: Automated, low-touch
**Focus**: Product-led growth with minimal human intervention

### 2. Hybrid (Agencies, E-commerce)

**Type**: Light-touch with assisted onboarding
**Focus**: Success through guidance and best practices

### 3. High-Touch (Enterprise)

**Type**: Dedicated, relationship-based
**Focus**: Strategic partnership and custom success

---

## Customer Acquisition Relationships

### First Impressions Matter

#### Landing Page Experience

**Principles**:

- Clear value proposition above the fold
- Social proof visible (logos, testimonials, reviews)
- Frictionless CTA (no credit card for trial)
- Transparent pricing

**A/B Testing Priorities**:

- Headline variants
- CTA button copy/color
- Social proof placement
- Pricing presentation

#### Free Trial Experience

**Goal**: Convert trial users to paid customers

**Trial Structure**:
| Day | Action | Purpose |
|-----|--------|---------|
| 0 | Welcome email + quick start guide | Immediate value |
| 1 | First success email (generate first article) | Quick win |
| 2 | Feature deep-dive email | Product discovery |
| 4 | Case study email | Social proof |
| 7 | Check-in + offer extension | Re-engagement |
| 10 | Urgency email (trial ending) | Conversion |
| 14 | Trial expired, special offer | Last chance |

**In-App Guidance**:

- Progressive onboarding
- Contextual tooltips
- Success milestones celebrated
- Objection handling at friction points

#### Demo Request Experience (Agencies/Enterprise)

**Process**:

1. **Instant Response**: Automated email + Calendly link
2. **Qualification**: Brief questionnaire (company size, use case, budget)
3. **Personalized Demo**: Customized to their specific needs
4. **Trial Setup**: Pre-configured trial account
5. **Follow-up Sequence**: 5-touch nurture sequence

**Demo Script Framework**:

- Discovery (5 min): Understand their challenges
- Solution (10 min): Show relevant features
- Proof (5 min): Share relevant case studies
- Social Proof (3 min): Show customer logos/reviews
- Next Steps (2 min): Trial setup or proposal

## Customer Retention Relationships

### Onboarding & Time to Value

#### SMB Onboarding (Self-Service)

**Time to First Value**: <15 minutes

**Steps**:

1. Sign up → 2 min
2. Connect CMS (optional) → 3 min
3. Run keyword discovery → 5 min
4. Generate first article → 5 min

**Success Milestones**:
| Milestone | Trigger | Celebration |
|-----------|---------|-------------|
| First article generated | API call complete | Confetti animation |
| First campaign created | Campaign saved | Tips popup |
| First content published | CMS publish detected | Email + badge |
| First ranking achieved | Rank tracker detects top 50 | Congratulations email |

#### Agency Onboarding (Light-Touch)

**Time to First Value**: <1 day

**Steps**:

1. Kickoff call (30 min) - Understand their workflows
2. Account setup (guided) - Team invites, branding
3. Training session (45 min) - Platform walkthrough
4. First client campaign (assisted) - Co-create first campaign
5. Check-in call (1 week later) - Optimization tips

**Resources Provided**:

- White-label setup guide
- Client pitch template
- Re-branded tutorial videos (optional)
- Proposal template with AutopilotRank included

#### Enterprise Onboarding (High-Touch)

**Time to First Value**: 2-4 weeks

**Phases**:

**Week 1: Discovery & Planning**

- Stakeholder interviews (marketing, legal, IT)
- Technical requirements assessment
- Integration planning (CMS, analytics, etc.)
- Success metrics definition

**Week 2: Setup & Configuration**

- Account provisioning
- SSO setup
- Custom integrations
- Brand guidelines configuration
- Approval workflow setup

**Week 3: Training & Enablement**

- Team training sessions (by role)
- Admin training
- Content creator training
- Approver training
- Documentation handoff

**Week 4: Pilot & Launch**

- Small pilot campaign (10-20 articles)
- Review and refinement
- Full launch
- Success celebration

### Ongoing Relationship Management

#### For SMB Customers (Automated)

**Touchpoints**:

- Weekly: In-app tips and feature discoveries
- Monthly: Newsletter with best practices
- Quarterly: Product updates email
- Annually: Anniversary email with impact report

**Automated Triggers**:
| Event | Trigger | Action |
|-------|---------|--------|
| Usage spike | 2x article generation | "What's working?" tips |
| Usage drop | <5 articles/week | Re-engagement email |
| Trial expansion | Feature usage detected | Advanced features email |
| Payment success | Monthly charge | Receipt + impact summary |
| Payment failed | Card declined | Friendly reminder + retry link |

#### For Agency Customers (Light-Touch)

**Touchpoints**:

- Monthly: Account manager email check-in
- Quarterly: Business review call (optional)
- Annually: Strategic planning session

**Proactive Outreach**:

- New feature announcements relevant to agencies
- Co-marketing opportunities
- Partner program updates
- Client success stories they can use

**Resources**:

- Monthly agency webinar
- Private Slack/Discord channel
- Template library (proposals, reports)
- Reseller toolkit

#### For Enterprise Customers (High-Touch)

**Dedicated Customer Success Manager (CSM)**

**Rhythm**:

- **Weekly**: Pulse check (Slack/Email)
- **Monthly**: Operational review (30 min)
- **Quarterly**: Business review (60-90 min)
- **Annually**: Strategic planning (half-day)

**Quarterly Business Review (QBR) Agenda**:

1. Executive summary (key achievements)
2. Usage metrics and trends
3. ROI analysis (traffic, rankings, revenue impact)
4. Success stories from the quarter
5. Product roadmap preview
6. Strategic initiatives for next quarter
7. Feedback and concerns

**Strategic Services**:

- Custom training for new team members
- Workflow optimization consulting
- Integration support
- Best practice documentation
- Executive sponsor alignment

## Customer Growth Relationships

### Upselling & Cross-selling

### Expansion Triggers

| Signal                    | Opportunity            | Action                |
| ------------------------- | ---------------------- | --------------------- |
| Article limit hit (80%+)  | Upgrade to next tier   | In-app prompt + email |
| Multiple team members     | Agency/Enterprise tier | CSM outreach          |
| API usage detected        | Enterprise plan        | Feature unlock offer  |
| Agency reselling          | Partner program        | Invitation to join    |
| Competitor features asked | Roadmap alignment      | Beta access           |

### Upgrade Paths

**From Free to Starter**:

- Trigger: 3 free articles used
- Offer: 20% off first month (time-limited)
- Emphasis: Articles they generated, what they could do with 30/mo

**From Starter to Growth**:

- Trigger: Article limit consistently hit (80%+)
- Offer: "3x the articles for 2x the price"
- Emphasis: GSC integration, advanced humanizer, higher batch limits

**From Growth to Agency**:

- Trigger: Team member limit or article limit consistently hit
- Offer: Annual payment discount (save 20%)
- Emphasis: 500 articles, white-label, team accounts, API access

**From Agency to Enterprise**:

- Trigger: SSO request, API needs, compliance requirements
- Offer: Custom pricing with CSM
- Emphasis: Priority support, custom integrations, SLA

### Cross-sell Opportunities

- **WordPress plugin users** → Main platform
- **Shopify app users** → E-commerce features
- **API users** → Full platform (if just using API)
- **Agencies** → White-label program

## Customer Advocacy Relationships

### Building Advocates

### Identify Potential Advocates

**Characteristics**:

- High usage (>100 articles/month)
- Long-term customer (6+ months)
- Positive sentiment (high NPS score)
- Results-driven (has success metrics)
- Industry influencer or agency owner

### Advocate Tiers

| Tier         | Criteria                | Benefits                          | Activities                          |
| ------------ | ----------------------- | --------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| **User**     | Any customer            | Basic support, product updates    | Reviews, referrals                  |
| **Champion** | 3+ months, positive NPS | Early access, beta features       | Case studies, testimonials          |
| **VIP**      | 12+ months, high impact | Exclusive access, direct feedback | Speaking, referrals, advisory board |

### Advocate Activities

**Case Studies**:

- Interview process (30-45 min call)
- Draft review and approval
- Published on website, email, social
- Incentive: 1 free month of service

**Video Testimonials**:

- Provide question guide
- Record via Zoom (15-20 min)
- Professional editing
- Incentive: $200 credit or 2 free months

**Referral Program**:

- Unique referral link
- 20% commission on first year revenue
- 3 months free for referred friend
- Dashboard to track referrals

**Guest Posting**:

- Opportunity to write for our blog
- Topic: How they use AutopilotRank
- Link back to their site
- Expanded reach and authority

**Speaking Opportunities**:

- Conference co-presentations
- Webinar co-hosting
- Podcast guest appearances
- Building their personal brand

## Churn Prevention Relationships

### Identifying At-Risk Customers

### Churn Signals

| Signal                | Risk Level  | Indicator                                    |
| --------------------- | ----------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| Usage decline         | Medium      | <50% of normal usage for 2+ weeks            |
| Support tickets       | Low-Medium  | Multiple unresolved issues                   |
| Payment failed        | High        | Declined card, not updated                   |
| Competitor mentioned  | Medium-High | Sales calls, cancellations citing competitor |
| Team member departure | Medium      | Key contact leaves company                   |
| Negative feedback     | High        | Low NPS, negative reviews                    |
| Feature gap           | Medium      | Repeated requests for missing feature        |

### Proactive Retention Strategies

#### For Usage Decline

**Automated Outreach**:

- "We noticed you haven't generated content in a while"
- Offer quick win: "Here are 5 keywords you could rank for"
- Re-engagement offer: "Come back with 50 bonus articles"

**Human Outreach** (Growth/Agency):

- CSM reaches out personally
- Understanding: "What changed?"
- Solution: Help them get unstuck

#### For Competitor Switching

**Competitive Win-Back**:

- Acknowledge the feedback
- Offer competitive comparison
- Provide exclusive offer to stay
- Address specific gaps with roadmap

#### For Payment Issues

**Graceful Handling**:

- Friendly reminder (not accusatory)
- Multiple retry attempts
- Flexible payment options
- Service continuation during resolution

### Win-Back Campaigns

**30-60-90 Day Post-Cancellation**:

| Timing  | Message                           | Offer                        |
| ------- | --------------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| 30 days | "We miss you - here's what's new" | Feature highlights, no offer |
| 60 days | "Special comeback offer"          | 25% off first 3 months       |
| 90 days | "Final offer - are you sure?"     | 50% off first month          |

## Relationship Infrastructure

### Technology Stack

**Customer Communication**:

- Intercom or Drift (in-app chat)
- Customer.io or Klaviyo (email automation)
- Notion (knowledge base)

**Customer Management**:

- HubSpot or Salesforce (CRM)
- Gainsight or Totango (customer success)
- Mixpanel or Amplitude (product analytics)

**Support**:

- Zendesk or Front (help desk)
- Slack (internal collaboration)
- Loom (video support)

### Team Structure (as we scale)

**Phase 1 (0-500 customers)**:

- Founder handles all relationships
- Community manager for Discord

**Phase 2 (500-2,000 customers)**:

- 1 Customer Success Manager
- 1 Support specialist
- Community manager

**Phase 3 (2,000-5,000 customers)**:

- 2-3 CSMs (segmented)
- 2 Support specialists
- Community manager
- Head of Customer Success

**Phase 4 (5,000+ customers)**:

- CSM team (segmented by tier)
- Support team (tiered support)
- Community team
- Customer Education team
- Head of Customer Success + managers
