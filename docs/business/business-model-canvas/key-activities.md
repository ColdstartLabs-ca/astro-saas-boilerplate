# Key Activities

## Overview

Key activities are the most important actions a company must take to make its business model work. For AutopilotRank, these activities span product development, customer acquisition, operations, and continuous improvement.

---

## 1. Product Development Activities

**Ongoing Activity**: Maintain and improve content generation quality

**Tasks**:

- Prompt engineering for better outputs

## Activity Prioritization Framework

### ICE Scoring (Impact, Confidence, Effort)

Used for prioritizing features, initiatives, and experiments.

| Score | Impact | Confidence | Effort              |
| ----- | ------ | ---------- | ------------------- |
| 1     | Low    | Low        | High (3+ months)    |
| 2     | Medium | Medium     | Medium (1-3 months) |
| 3     | High   | High       | Low (<1 month)      |

**Priority**: Multiply I × C × E, prioritize highest scores

### RICE Prioritization

Alternative framework for larger initiatives.

**RICE Score** = (Reach × Impact × Confidence) / Effort

- **Reach**: How many customers affected?
- **Impact**: How much impact per customer? (1-3)
- **Confidence**: How confident are we? (%)
- **Effort**: How many person-months?
